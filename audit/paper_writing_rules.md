# Paper writing rules (Kenta, 22 Nov 2026)

These apply to every paper in this project and to future papers. Use them together with `how_to_research_correctly.md`.

1. **One story.** The abstract tells the whole story in this order:
   - the reason for the paper (the research gap);
   - what we do (the method);
   - the general result, with only the main numbers (two or three, not every statistic);
   - what it contributes to the community;
   - its novelty.
   The body then tells the same story in more detail, in the same order. Every section should advance it.
2. **Roadmap.** End the Introduction with a short paragraph saying what each following section does.
3. **Section openers.** Begin each section with one or two sentences saying what the section is for. This matters most when the section has several subsections; say what each one covers.
4. **Numbers.** Detailed numbers, confidence intervals and rank changes go in the body, not the abstract. Every number in the body must still trace to a logged run, as `how_to_research_correctly.md` requires.
5. **Page limit.** Apply these rules within the venue's page limit. Shorten other text rather than drop a roadmap or section opener.

Checklist before handing a draft to Kenta:
- [ ] The abstract covers gap → method → main result → contribution → novelty, with at most about three numbers.
- [ ] The Introduction ends with a roadmap.
- [ ] Every section has an opening paragraph.
- [ ] The conclusion restates the same story (result, contribution, recommendation).
